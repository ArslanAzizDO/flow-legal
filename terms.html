The app

Flow visualises audio playing on your device. It is provided as-is, without warranty. Some apps and services block playback capture; when that happens Flow receives silence rather than sound, and that is a limitation of those apps, not a fault you can fix.

Free and premium

Flow is free to install and includes three visualizers at no cost, for as long as you keep the app. The remaining visualizers require Flow Premium, available as a subscription or as a one-time lifetime unlock.

  • Cancel or manage a subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling stops future charges; it does not refund the current period.
